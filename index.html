(02) - Process · Four steps, one team

From first sketch to the reveal.

A Fifty5 living room in Rochdale, filmed on site during the fit and after.

01

Consultation

We visit, measure up and listen to how you actually use the space.

  • A home visit to see and measure the room
  • Your brief, your style and your budget
  • Ideas talked through on the spot
02

Design

Drawings, material samples and 3D visuals until every detail feels right.

  • Space plan and wall elevations
  • Material and finish samples
  • 3D visuals to sign off before we make
03

Making

Built by hand in our own workshop and finished to order.

  • Cut and assembled in our workshop
  • Finished to order, by hand
  • Wiring and lighting planned in
04

Install & reveal

Fitted by the people who made it, then handed over spotless.

  • Fitted by the people who made it
  • Lighting and tech set up and tested
  • Handed over clean and ready to use
